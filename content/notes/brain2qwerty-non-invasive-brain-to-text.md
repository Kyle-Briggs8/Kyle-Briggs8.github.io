## Summary

- Brain2Qwerty v2 is a non-invasive AI system from Meta that decodes brain activity into text in real-time, achieving 61% word accuracy (up to 78% for best participants).
- This performance significantly surpasses previous non-invasive methods (8% accuracy) and approaches the accuracy levels of surgical brain implants.
- The system utilizes end-to-end deep learning and fine-tunes large language models on magnetoencephalography (MEG) data collected from volunteers.
- Meta AI is open-sourcing the training code and its partner, BCBL, is releasing the v1 dataset to accelerate neuroscience research and aid individuals with communication impairments.
- Accuracy improves log-linearly with data volume, indicating potential for further performance gains through data scaling, as part of Meta's broader Digital Brain Project.
