## ⚡ TL;DR

The best one-hour mental model of what an LLM actually is: two files (weights + code), a lossy compression of the internet, and an "operating system" in the making.

## Summary

*Summary is AI-drafted — machine voice, not mine.*

- Demystifies the stack: pretraining as compression of internet text into weights, finetuning as swapping the dataset for curated Q&A, RLHF as preference polish.
- The "LLM OS" framing — the model as a kernel orchestrating tools, memory, and peripherals — predicted much of the agent era.
- Closes with security: jailbreaks, prompt injection, and data poisoning presented as the new OS-security frontier.
