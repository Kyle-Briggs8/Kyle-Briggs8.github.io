## ⚡ TL;DR

Startups don't take off by themselves — founders recruit users manually, over-deliver on delight, and do embarrassingly unscalable things that look like weakness but are actually the engine.

## Summary

*Summary is AI-drafted — machine voice, not mine.*

- The Airbnb door-to-door story as the archetype: the fragile, manual early phase isn't a detour from the "real" startup, it *is* the startup.
- Manual-first has a second payoff — doing things by hand teaches you what to build before you automate the wrong thing.
- The mental trick: think of growth as compounding from a tiny deliberate fire, not as launching a rocket.
