## ⚡ TL;DR

The internet's zero distribution cost means power shifts from controlling supply to owning demand — the single framework that explains Google, Amazon, Netflix, and most of what VCs fund against.

## Summary

*Summary is AI-drafted — machine voice, not mine.*

- Pre-internet value chains were won by controlling scarce distribution; the internet made distribution free, so winners now aggregate *users* and commoditize suppliers.
- The flywheel: better experience wins users → users attract suppliers → more supply improves the experience. Winner-take-most follows.
- Reading big-tech strategy, antitrust cases, or a startup's "marketplace" pitch without this lens is playing chess without knowing how the queen moves.
