- Vanguard was founded by Jack Bogle in 1974, stemming from a 'revenge plot' after he was fired by his partners.
- It operates on a unique mutual ownership model, where clients who own Vanguard funds also own the firm itself.
- This structure means Vanguard has no shareholders and makes no profits, instead returning excess margins to clients via lower fees.
- Since 1975, this model has saved retail investors approximately $500 billion by reducing Wall Street management fees.
- Vanguard has grown to be the single largest equity owner in most S&P 500 corporations, serving 50 million clients.
