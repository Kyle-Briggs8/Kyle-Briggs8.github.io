## ⚡ TL;DR

The canonical taxonomy of LLM agents: planning, memory, and tool use as the three pillars, written before the ecosystem had settled on vocabulary.

## Summary

*Summary is AI-drafted — machine voice, not mine.*

- Frames an agent as LLM-as-controller with three components: **planning** (task decomposition, self-reflection like ReAct and Reflexion), **memory** (short-term in-context vs. long-term retrieval from vector stores), and **tool use** (extending the model beyond its weights).
- Surveys the early case studies (AutoGPT, GenerativeAgents) with honest attention to failure modes: finite context, unreliable natural-language interfaces, planning brittleness.
- Still one of the best mental scaffolds for the field — most later frameworks are elaborations of this post's diagram.
